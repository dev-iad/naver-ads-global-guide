GET STARTED

Launch your first ad in 4 steps

No complicated process. Just follow along.

Create an ad account

Sign up for Naver Search Ads with a Naver ID and register your business information.

Set up campaigns & ad groups

Choose a campaign type that fits your goal, then set a daily budget, regions and schedule.

Add keywords & creatives

Pick the keywords customers search for and write a compelling title and description.

Set bids & charge Biz Money

Set your bid and top up Biz Money. Once reviewed, your ad goes live.

PRO TIPS

Spend less, perform better

01

Start with specific keywords

Long-tail keywords with clear purchase intent usually cost less per conversion than broad, highly competitive ones.

02

Test 2–3 creatives at a time

Vary titles and descriptions, compare click-through rates, and keep only the winners.

03

Bid for profit, not for rank

Being #1 isn't the goal. Find the position where your cost per acquisition (CPA) hits your target.

04

Always set up conversion tracking

Track purchases, inquiries and bookings so you know which keywords actually make money.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to get started?

You set your own daily budget, so you can start small. Check Naver's official guidance for the minimum Biz Money top-up and product-specific requirements.

How am I charged?

Search ads generally use cost-per-click (CPC) billing: you pay only when your ad is clicked. Impressions alone are not charged.

How long does ad approval take?

Creatives are reviewed before they go live. Review time can vary by industry and wording.

Can I run ads without an agency?

Absolutely. Follow the basic steps in this guide, start small, and scale up as you learn from the data.

Do I need a Korean business registration?

Requirements depend on the account type and product. Please confirm eligibility for overseas advertisers on the official Naver Search Ads site.
