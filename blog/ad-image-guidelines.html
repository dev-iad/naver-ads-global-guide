Ad Image Guidelines: What Makes an Image Approvable

Your image is the first thing people see, and the first thing reviewers check. Follow three simple principles and most rejections disappear.

NNaver Ads Guide Team
🖼️
IN SHORT
  • Appropriate: no copyright issues, nothing that breaks the law or social norms.
  • High quality: sharp, natural, unedited-looking photos work best.
  • Relevant: the image must match your business, your product and your keyword.

1Use an appropriate image

This is the baseline. If an image fails here, nothing else matters.

Not allowed

  • Images that may infringe copyright or portrait rights.
  • Images against laws, public morals or social order: sexual, violent, or suggestive of sexual content.

Safe choice

  • Photos you took yourself.
  • Images you own or are licensed to use.

2Use a high-quality image

Naver recommends high-quality photos you took yourself. High-quality graphics and illustrations are also fine. Low-quality ones are not.

The "do not use" list

  1. Low-quality graphics or illustrations.
  2. Added company CI, logo or mark. Generally not allowed. The exception: a logo that is part of the product or service itself and doesn't lower the overall quality of the image.
  3. Low resolution, heavy retouching or broken images.
  4. Composite or split images, extra margins, and text overlays.
  5. Borders or other added effects.
  6. Stretched or distorted images (width or height pulled out of proportion).
  7. Phone numbers, web addresses or emails. Not allowed when they are visible in the image. Exception: a storefront photo where the number appears but isn't emphasized.
  8. The same image as another approved ad for the same keyword. This applies no matter which advertiser owns the other ad.
  9. Duplicate images in image-type sublinks. If any of the 3 images repeat, exposure is limited.
💡

Pro tip: A clean, well-lit photo of your real product or storefront beats a polished banner nearly every time. Put your message in the ad title and description, not in the image.

3Use a relevant image

Reviewers ask one question: does this image belong with this ad? Make sure it connects to all three of the following.

🌐

Your business

Related to your website and company.

📦

Your offer

Related to the products and services you provide.

🔎

Your ad

Related to the keyword and the ad copy (title, description).

Image checklist before you upload

  • I own it, or I'm licensed to use it.
  • It is sharp, undistorted and free of borders, logos, text and phone numbers.
  • It shows my real product, service or business.
  • It fits my keyword and ad copy.
  • It isn't the same image as another approved ad for this keyword.
