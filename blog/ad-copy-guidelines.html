Ad Copy Guidelines: Phrases You Need to Be Careful With

"Best," "official," "award-winning." They sound great, and they are exactly the words that get ads rejected. Here is how to use them without getting stuck in review.

NNaver Ads Guide Team
🛡️
IN SHORT
  • Big claims are allowed, but only if you can prove them.
  • Proof means your website shows it, or you submit an official document.
  • False or exaggerated claims get your ad pulled.
  • Past awards must be listed with the year.

Three kinds of claims that get checked

Naver reviews any wording that makes your business sound bigger, better or more official than the competition. Three types come up most often.

🏆

Superlatives

Claims that you rank above everyone else.

"Best" · "No. 1" · "Top"
🔒

Exclusivity

Claims of official status or sole rights.

"Official" · "Exclusive" · "Sole distributor" · "Only"
🎖️

Recognition

Claims of certification or awards.

"Certified" · "Designated" · "Selected" · "Award-winning"

The one rule behind all of them

The good news: all three types follow the same two-part rule. Learn it once and you are covered.

  1. Make it checkable. The claim must be visible on your advertised website, or you submit an objective document from a credible authority.
  2. Be ready to back it up. If someone reports your wording as false or exaggerated, you may be asked for supporting evidence. If it turns out to be false or exaggerated, the ad can no longer run.
If you can't show it, don't say it.

Using award wording the right way

Awards come with a few extra requirements. Your website or the documents you submit must clearly show all three of the following:

🏛️

Who

The awarding organization.

🏷️

What

The award category.

📅

When

The timing of the award.

Is your award still current, or is it history?

This is where many advertisers slip up. A "past" award is one that another company has since won in the same category. If that is you, the ad description must include the year.

✅ Still valid

  • List the award on its own.
  • No year is needed.

🕰️ From the past

  • Show the award and the year together.
  • Leaving out the year can get the ad rejected.
💡

Pro tip: Keep a folder with your award certificates, rankings and official letters. If a review or a report ever comes in, you can send proof the same day.

Before you submit your ad

  • Can visitors see this claim on my website?
  • Do I have an official document to back it up?
  • For awards: are the organization, category and timing clear?
  • Is the award from the past? If so, did I add the year?
