Ad Copy Guidelines: Phrases You Need to Be Careful With

Some claims in your ad creatives require proof before Naver will approve them. Here is what you need to know about superlatives, exclusivity claims and awards.

Superlative claims

Superlative expressions (such as "best" or "No. 1") may be used only under the following conditions.

  1. The claim can be verified on the advertised website, or you submit an objective document issued by a credible authority.
  2. If a report is filed saying the wording is false or exaggerated, you may be asked to submit supporting evidence. If the claim turns out to be false or exaggerated, the ad can no longer run.

"Official," "exclusive," "sole distributor," "only" and similar claims

Words that signal official status or exclusivity may be used only under the following conditions.

  1. The claim can be verified on the advertised website, or you submit an objective document issued by a credible authority.
  2. If a report is filed saying the wording is false or exaggerated, you may be asked to submit supporting evidence. If the claim turns out to be false or exaggerated, the ad can no longer run.

"Certified," "designated," "selected," "award-winning" and similar claims

Words that refer to certification, designation, selection or awards may be used only under the following conditions.

  1. The claim can be verified on the advertised website, or you submit an objective document issued by a credible authority.
  2. If a report is filed saying the wording is false or exaggerated, you may be asked to submit supporting evidence. If the claim turns out to be false or exaggerated, the ad can no longer run.

Guide for award-related claims

To include award-related wording, your website or the documents you submit must clearly show all of the following:

  • The awarding organization
  • The award category
  • When the award was given

If the award is from the past rather than current, you must include the year of the award in the ad description.

Award is still valid: you may list the award on its own.

Award is from the past: you must list the year together with the award.

A "past" award means that another company has since won the same award in the same category.
